Current Version

Version v.1.21

A character built straight onto a token now stays there. That one fault was also why the GM's Party panel could look empty and why rests never arrived. A finished character can now be edited from inside Owlbear too, so Growth granted at the table can actually be spent.

Player changelog vs. technical log

This page is the player-facing changelog: it explains what changed in practical play terms. For implementation details, data schema changes, compatibility notes and developer handoff context, use the technical notes.

Open the technical upgrade notes.

Injuries Are Visible

Taking, healing, reopening or renaming an Injury posts to the log. Injuries still change no numbers — they are a Truth about your character, and that has not changed.

Party Panel

GM only. Every character on a token, with their Spirit and whether they have caught up with the rests you have called.

Caught Up, Behind, Not Synced

Three states, not two. A brand new character reads as Not synced rather than Behind, because it has nothing to catch up on.

Five-Part Sheet

Character, Actions, Items, Growth, Share Code. The same sections in the same order, grouped so the page is easier to move around.

Tidier Item Block

Starting Equipment and Item Catalogue start collapsed. Open one and it stays open while you work.

Shared Roll Room Removed

It needed a server that was never set up. Rolls have travelled through Owlbear since v1.12, which needs no server at all.

The Rolls and Actions log and the Party panel are Owlbear Rodeo features. In a normal browser tab the creator works as it always has, with rolls and abilities shown to you alone. Version 1.21 needs extension 0.9.0; the install link is unchanged, so nobody needs to reinstall.
Version History

Newest first. Click any version to open it.

The shared table

v.1.21 Characters stay on their tokens, and a sheet in play can be edited
  • Building a brand-new character straight onto a token now sticks. Pressing Finalize saves it to the token the way pasting a code always has. Before this it looked finished, played correctly, and was gone the moment you closed the sheet, because it had never actually been written to the token.
  • This is also why the GM's Party panel could read empty and why rests and scene boundaries never arrived: both go looking for characters on tokens, and a character that was never saved is not there to find.
  • A finished character can now be edited from inside Owlbear. The step bar — Origin, Archetype, Growth and the rest — appears when you press Edit Character, so you can go straight to the step you want.
  • Added a Spend Growth button to the Growth section of the sheet. When the GM grants Growth mid-session, that is one click to the right screen instead of hunting for it.
  • While editing a finished character, every step offers "Done — back to play", so you are never walked through the whole wizard to get back to the sheet. Returning to play changes nothing you have spent or taken.
  • A rest the GM called while you were part-way through editing now lands as soon as you press Done, rather than waiting for the next thing to happen in the room.
  • Extension 0.9.0 is unchanged and still required. No reinstall.
v.1.20 Injuries in the log, a party panel for the GM, and a sheet in five parts
  • Taking, healing, reopening, changing or removing an Injury now posts to the Rolls and Actions log, so the table sees who got hurt and who got patched up. Damage was the last consequential thing at the table that happened silently.
  • Injuries still have no mechanical effect of their own. Nothing here changes a number: an Injury is a Truth representing harm, and the Spirit you spend to avoid one was already logged.
  • Renaming an Injury — which is how the rules treat one, turning Bleeding into Bandaged Wound — posts once when you finish typing, not once per letter.
  • Heal and Treat are the same control. Pressing Heal moves the Injury into Healed Injuries and says so in the log.
  • Added a Party panel to the roller, visible only to the GM. It lists every character on a token in the scene with their Spirit and whether they have caught up with the rests and scene boundaries you have called.
  • A character reads as Caught up, Behind, or Not synced. Behind names what it is waiting on, so "Waiting on Bed" tells you who to chase. Not synced means the character has never met this room and has nothing to catch up on — a new character is not behind.
  • Opening the roller with a token already selected now reads that token straight away. It used to show nothing until you clicked something else.
  • The play sheet is now five clearly separated sections: Character, Actions Talents & Abilities, Items and Equipment, Growth, and Share Code. Nothing moved relative to anything else — the same sections in the same order, grouped so the page reads as five things instead of one long column.
  • Character Share Code is its own titled section at the foot of the sheet.
  • Starting Equipment and Item Catalogue now start collapsed. Open one and it stays open while you work.
  • Removed the Shared Roll Room. It needed a server that was never set up, and its Connect button could only ever fail. Rolls have reached the table through Owlbear since v1.12, which needs no server at all.
  • Requires extension 0.9.0. The install link is unchanged, so no reinstall is needed.
v.1.19B Nanobarrier fix and catch-up improvements
  • Fixed Nanobarrier doing nothing. Sentinels are granted it automatically, and the sheet was not counting an automatically granted talent as one you have, so every press was silently ignored and the cost never moved off Free.
  • Sheets now catch up the moment they are opened, instead of waiting until you rolled or pressed something.
  • When your sheet catches up, it posts to the log so the table can see who has received a rest and who has not.
  • The catch-up entry carries the detail — Spirit regained, items recharged — rather than only naming what the GM called.
  • A rest that restores no Spirit no longer says "regained 0 Spirit".
  • GM table control buttons now ask for confirmation. The first press arms the button, the second sends it, and it disarms itself after a few seconds.
  • Adjusting Momentum or Threat with the roller's own buttons now posts to the log, attributed to whoever pressed them.
  • Requires extension 0.8.1.
v.1.19 GM table controls
  • Added a Table Controls panel to the roller, visible only to the GM, with buttons for Breather, Break and Bed rests and for End Scene, New Session and New Adventure.
  • Pressing one applies it to every attached character, not just the sheets that happen to be open.
  • A character whose sheet was closed catches up the next time it is opened, and is told what was called.
  • Catching up after several missed presses applies the strongest one once. Three missed Bed rests is one Bed rest.
  • A new character attached to a token does not retroactively take rests it was never present for.
  • The GM calling a rest appears once in the log, attributed to the GM, rather than once per character.
  • Requires extension 0.8.0. The install link is unchanged, so no reinstall is needed.
v.1.18 Nanobarrier, damage buttons, and item charge logging
  • Added Nanobarrier as a tracked ability with all three options — Barrier, Shock and Display — each showing what the next activation will cost.
  • Nanobarrier's first use in a scene is free, the second costs 1 Threat, and each use after that costs one more. All three options draw on the same count.
  • The cost resets at End Scene, and therefore also at New Session and New Adventure.
  • Replaced the single extra-damage button with six, one per Momentum, labelled so both the cost and the damage are visible before you commit.
  • Damage buttons show doubled damage for characters with Precision Firepower, with a note naming the talent responsible.
  • Discharging or recharging a Powered item now posts to the Rolls and Actions log.
  • A rest posts a single summary line rather than one entry per item recharged.
v.1.17 Rolls and Actions log, item buttons, and Start Over
  • The shared roll log became the Rolls and Actions log: it now records ability uses, item uses, and every change to the Momentum and Threat pools alongside rolls.
  • Log entries name who acted and what they used, so "Kell — Second Wind — spent 3 Momentum, regained 2 Spirit" reads as one line instead of a number moving on its own.
  • Added buttons for the five items whose rules carry a cost: Communicator, Tactical Lens, Combat Automed and Emergency Trauma Kit add Threat; Combat Medkit spends Momentum. These were previously notes asking you to move the counter yourself.
  • An item button you cannot currently afford is shown greyed out rather than hidden, so you can still see the item has the ability.
  • Manual adjustments to the Threat counter now post to the log.
  • Added a Start Over button beside Save Local on a finished sheet. It warns first if the character has not been saved locally.
  • Moved Starting Equipment above the Item Catalogue in Inventory & Equipment.
  • Removed the Shared Roll Room panel, which relied on a server that is not going to be available.
  • Confirmed that New Adventure refreshes I Know a Guy, which it already did.

Owlbear Rodeo

v.1.16 One sheet, and no more loading from elsewhere
  • The Owlbear character sheet and the character creator became a single page, so the sheet you play on and the sheet you build on can no longer drift apart.
  • Fixed: opening a character from a token could quietly fall back to the ordinary creator, showing the import screen instead of your character. Anything you changed was saved to your browser rather than to the token, and your rolls and Momentum never reached the table.
  • Fixed: rolls made from the sheet were reaching the shared log without their Difficulty, so they always read as if you had rolled against Difficulty 0.
  • The creator is now completely self-contained. It loads nothing from anywhere, which means it also works offline and from a saved copy on your own machine.
v.1.15 Threat goes where it is supposed to go
  • Adrenaline Rush and I Know a Guy no longer just tell you to add Threat. Played inside Owlbear Rodeo, they add it to the table's Threat pool for you.
  • There is a Threat counter beside your other resources when you are playing on a token, showing the table's current Threat. Use the plus button for anything else that tells you to add Threat, such as a Communicator message or a Tactical Lens signal.
  • Momentum on an Owlbear sheet is the group's pool, not a private counter. Spending it in any way — the buttons, the number field, or an ability — moves the shared total.

In a normal browser tab nothing changes: the sheet still tells you the number and leaves the pool to your Game Master, because there is no shared table to write to.

v.1.14 Quicker rolling, and dice that tell you the result
  • Click any Attribute or Skill to select it for a test. The one you picked is highlighted, and an Attribute shut down by Exhaustion cannot be picked.
  • The Dice Roller moved up to sit directly beneath your Attributes, Skills and Resources, instead of near the bottom of the sheet.
  • There is a Difficulty setting next to the Roll button. Your result now says whether the test passed and how much Momentum you gained, rather than leaving you to work it out.
  • The share code block leads with Copy Code and Save Local, and the code itself sits in a small scrolling box below rather than filling the page.
  • Momentum earned on a roll is reported, not spent for you. You can use it straight away or save it to the group pool, up to the usual maximum of 6.
v.1.13 The last of the sheet travels with your code
  • Your temperament's Drive and Attitude now travel with your code, alongside the exhaustion trigger that was already there.
  • Your archetype goal, your starting equipment list and your origin's special note come across too.
  • Coin, Growth and Momentum maxima are included, along with the "equipped +N" footnote that explains where a raised maximum came from.
  • Powered items carry their recharge timing, so a tabletop can tell a Breather item from a Bed one.
  • Codes stopped growing with your inventory. They sit at roughly 10 kB no matter how much gear you carry.
v.1.12 Abilities, descriptions and exhaustion in your code
  • Exhaustion states travel with your code. A tabletop that reads it can show all four, let you toggle them, and grey out the attribute each one shuts down.
  • Your origin abilities now come across with their full rules text, so you can read what they do without opening the creator.
  • Origin, archetype and temperament descriptions are included, along with your temperament's exhaustion clause.
  • Bonds now carry the bond type's name and description rather than just a label.
  • Codes grew to around 9 kB. Older codes still work; you just will not get exhaustion tracking until you export a fresh one.
v.1.11 Character codes a virtual tabletop can read
  • Your code now includes your finished numbers: attributes, skills, Spirit and Supply maxima, tech level, your talents and your equipment with damage, injury, protection and qualities.
  • This means another tool can display your character without needing a copy of the rules. Paste your code in once and it knows who you are.
  • Everything you change during play — Spirit spent, injuries taken, truths written, gear discharged — can be carried back here by pasting the code in again. Nothing is lost in the trip.
  • Codes roughly doubled in length. Older codes still load normally, and codes made from this version onward still work in older copies of the creator.
