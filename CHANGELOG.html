Current Version

Version v.1.30

A room with no GM now says so, instead of quietly dropping every change to Momentum and Threat. The raised Complication range stands out in Threat's colour. The pop-out window still needs work, but it now tells you what went wrong.

Player changelog vs. technical log

This page is the player-facing changelog: it explains what changed in practical play terms. For implementation details, data schema changes, compatibility notes and developer handoff context, use the technical notes.

Open the technical upgrade notes.

Injuries Are Visible

Taking, healing, reopening or renaming an Injury posts to the log. Injuries still change no numbers — they are a Truth about your character, and that has not changed.

Party Panel

GM only. Every character on a token, with their Spirit and whether they have caught up with the rests you have called.

Caught Up, Behind, Not Synced

Three states, not two. A brand new character reads as Not synced rather than Behind, because it has nothing to catch up on.

Five-Part Sheet

Character, Actions, Items, Growth, Share Code. The same sections in the same order, grouped so the page is easier to move around.

Tidier Item Block

Starting Equipment and Item Catalogue start collapsed. Open one and it stays open while you work.

Sheet in Its Own Window

Press Pop out and the sheet becomes a real browser window you can move, resize, or put on a second screen. The map and the log stay where they are.

Recent Rolls

Your last three rolls stay on the sheet after you close it, and travel with your character code.

Maverick Drive

Fires by itself when the GM spends 3 or more Threat at once. The Spirit arrives, the log says so, and closed sheets catch up.

One Change, One Line

A run of +/− presses on Threat or Momentum is added up and posted once. The number moves as you press; it reads dimmed until the table confirms it.

Rivalry Bonds

When someone uses Adrenaline Rush, anyone holding a rivalry bond with them recovers 1 Spirit. It arrives on its own.

Supportive Bonds

Name the ally you are helping and a supportive bond with them adds +1 to what they get, on Second Wind and at a rest.

Sharing a Rest

Give up part of the Spirit a rest just returned to you and hand it to an ally. New — the sheet never had this before.

Shared Roll Room Removed

It needed a server that was never set up. Rolls have travelled through Owlbear since v1.12, which needs no server at all.

The Rolls and Actions log and the Party panel are Owlbear Rodeo features. In a normal browser tab the creator works as it always has, with rolls and abilities shown to you alone. Version 1.30 needs extension 0.9.10; the install link is unchanged, so nobody needs to reinstall — but everyone should reload the room once.
Version History

The shared table

v.1.30 A room with no GM says so
  • A room with no GM now warns you. Without a GM present, nothing saves to the room — Momentum, Threat, rests and the shared log all need the GM's connection to write them. You could add Momentum, watch your own sheet change, and never see the pool move. It still works that way, but the roller and the sheet both say so now instead of looking broken.
  • The warning clears by itself the moment a GM joins or someone is promoted.
  • A raised Complication range is now in Threat's colour, on its own line under the rest of the dice hint. It wrapped to two lines anyway, so the break is somewhere useful — and playing on the wrong odds because you did not notice the change is worth avoiding.
  • The pop-out window is still not working, and this release does not fix it. What it does is tell you why it failed. Most likely your Owlbear room is still running the older extension: reload the room, then try again. The window now says which of the three possible causes it hit, and offers a Try again button.
  • Requires extension 0.9.10. Install link unchanged, so no reinstall — but reload the room once.
v.1.29 The sheet in its own window, and it remembers your rolls
  • The sheet can open in its own browser window. Open it from the token as usual, then press Pop out. It becomes a normal window you can move, resize, or drag to a second screen — and the map, the log and the roller all stay where they are. No more opening the sheet to roll, closing it to see the log, and opening it again.
  • Everything works from that window: rolls reach the table, Threat and Momentum move the shared pools, edits save back to the token.
  • Popping out closes the sheet inside Owlbear on purpose. Two copies of one character open at once would each keep overwriting the other.
  • If your browser blocks the window, the button tells you so and nothing else changes — allow pop-ups for Owlbear Rodeo and press it again.
  • If you close Owlbear while the window is still open, the window says plainly that it has lost the room rather than quietly doing nothing.
  • Your last three rolls now stay on the sheet. They used to vanish the moment you closed it. They are kept with the character, so they are still there when you reopen it and they travel with your character code. Each one is shown as it read when you made it, even if the GM has changed the Complication range since.
  • Requires extension 0.9.9. Install link unchanged, so no reinstall — but reload the room once.
v.1.28B The Maverick drive fires by itself
  • “When the GM spends 3 or more Threat at once, regain 1 Spirit” now happens on its own. Every Maverick at the table gets the Spirit, whether their sheet is open or not — a closed one catches up the next time it is opened, the same way rests do.
  • Nothing happens quietly. The GM sees “Spent 3 Threat at once” when they do it. Each Maverick gets their own message and a line in the shared log, marked Drive so it is not mistaken for a bond.
  • “At once” means at once. Two presses is two; two presses, a pause, then two more is still two runs and not a spend of four. Adding Threat never counts — only spending it.
  • This is the one drive the sheet can see. The other five — being first to act in a round, creating a scheming Truth, and so on — are things it has no way of knowing about, so they stay yours to claim.
  • Requires extension 0.9.8B. Install link unchanged, so no reinstall — but reload the room once.

1.28B fixes, from play:

  • “Add Momentum” in the roller is yours to press again. It was clickable for the GM and nobody else. The roller offers a roll's surplus to whoever made it, and rolls sent from a character sheet had never said who that was — so if you roll from your sheet, as most of us do, the button was never yours. Rolls made in the roller itself were always fine, which is why it took a while to spot.
  • Rolls already sitting in the log from before this fix can be claimed by anyone, since there is no way to tell whose they were.
v.1.27 Pressing + three times is one change, not three
  • A run of Threat or Momentum presses is added up and posted once. Raising Threat by 3 used to write three separate “added 1 Threat” lines, so the log recorded the clicking rather than the decision and everyone had to add them up. It now reads added 3 Threat, once.
  • Press + then − and nothing is posted at all. Previously that was two lines for a change nobody made.
  • The number still moves the moment you press it. While it is waiting for the table to confirm it, it reads dimmed with a dotted underline — the value is right, and the panel is not pretending it has landed yet.
  • Abilities are never rolled into a manual adjustment. Anything with its own name — Adrenaline Rush, Nanobarrier, Second Wind — still posts on its own, with its own wording. Two uses of Second Wind stay two entries.
  • Wait a moment between presses and you get two entries, as you should. Only a quick run counts as one decision.
  • Maverick players: your drive is “when the GM spends 3 or more Threat at once, regain 1 Spirit”, and until now there was no way to tell 3 at once from three separate 1s. The log now says removed 3 Threat in one line. It is still yours to claim — all six drives work that way, and several of them are things the sheet cannot see at all.
  • Requires extension 0.9.7. Install link unchanged, so no reinstall — but reload the room once.
v.1.26 Bonds pay out by themselves, and you can share a rest
  • Rivalry bonds now pay out. When a character uses Adrenaline Rush, everyone who holds a rivalry bond naming them recovers 1 Spirit. It lands on their sheet on its own — the next time they open it, if it was closed.
  • Worth reading twice, because it runs the opposite way to how it sounds: the person spending the Threat needs no bond at all. The bond belongs to the one who benefits. If you wrote a rivalry with Kestrel, you gain when Kestrel buys Spirit with Threat.
  • Second Wind now asks who you are helping. It used to spend the Momentum and tell nobody, which meant the ally had to be told out loud and the bond could not be worked out at all. A supportive bond with the ally you name adds +1 Spirit for them, automatically.
  • New: give up part of your rest to an ally. After any rest you may hand some of the Spirit you just regained to someone else, which is the rules' other route for a supportive bond — and something the sheet has never been able to do. The control appears under the rest buttons and lapses at the next rest or scene.
  • With the Performer talent you may split a rest between several allies. Without it, one per rest, as written.
  • Cautious now works. It adds +1 Spirit to a Second Wind you take on yourself. It has been in the rules text since the beginning and had never actually done anything.
  • Both the Second Wind and the rest-sharing box suggest the names of characters attached to tokens in your scene, so you can pick the exact spelling. Bonds only fire on an exact name, ignoring capitals and stray spaces — so if a bond is not paying out, check the spelling against the character's name first.
  • The Adrenaline Rush message used to say "your rivalry-bonded ally also gains 1 Spirit" based on your bonds, which was the rule backwards and contradicted the Actions entry three inches above it.
  • Requires extension 0.9.6. Install link unchanged, so no reinstall — but reload the room once.
v.1.25B The log finally catches everything, spare Momentum is a button, and the GM can raise the danger
  • End Scene, New Session and New Adventure now post to the log. They only ever showed you a popup, so the rest of the table never knew.
  • Spending Momentum now posts to the log too. Ask a Question, Counterattack, Damage, Reduce Time, Follow-Up and Second Wind all moved the pool silently. The underlying cause was one shared fault, so this class of miss should be gone rather than fixed one button at a time.
  • Writing, changing or removing a Truth or a Knowledge Fragment is now logged, the way Injuries have been. It posts once when you finish typing, not once per letter.
  • A roll that beats its Difficulty offers its spare Momentum as a button, on the sheet and in the roller. Press it to add that Momentum to the group pool; it greys out once taken so the same surplus cannot be counted twice. In the roller only the person who rolled, or the GM, can press it.
  • The GM can lower the Complication range from Table Controls: 20, 19, 18, 17, 16 or 15. Any die at or above the number complicates, for everyone, until it is set back. The dice hint on the roller says which number is live so nobody plays on the wrong odds.
  • Ending a scene now costs the group 1 Momentum, as the rules say. It comes off once, when the GM presses End Scene — not once per character.
  • Momentum is labelled Group Pool on the sheet, so it is clear it is not yours alone.
  • Knowledge Fragments have their own section now, just under the character. They used to sit inside the Resources grid, which pushed the dice roller away from the Attributes and Skills you pick a roll from.
  • Tooltips have room to breathe between the title and the text.
  • Fixed the double line under every section heading. One of the two rules had been drawn by mistake for several versions.
  • Requires extension 0.9.5B. Install link unchanged, so no reinstall — but reload the room once.

1.25B fixes, from testing 1.25:

  • The tooltip gap now works on every tooltip, and is much smaller. Most tooltip titles are written in a way that ignored the spacing entirely, so only a handful of them had picked it up.
  • Table Controls: Complication on is now Complication Level, with room enough not to wrap.
  • Knowledge Fragments collapses, and no longer repeats its own name as a subheading underneath itself.
  • Fixed the remaining double line, under Actions, Talents & Abilities. The 1.25 fix only covered one kind of section and that block uses another.
v.1.24 The GM sees hidden rolls, and the sheet loses three things it did not need
  • A player's Hidden roll is now visible to the GM. It is hidden from the other players, not from the GM — which is how it should have worked from the start. Everyone else still sees only that a roll was made, and the label you typed if you gave one.
  • Worth knowing, and we would rather say it plainly: Hidden conceals a result from other players' screens, not from a player who goes looking in their browser's developer tools. Owlbear gives extensions no private channel, so a roll that reaches the GM reaches everyone's browser. If a result must be unreachable, use Secret — that one is never sent anywhere.
  • The mode note under the Roll button now says so, rather than implying the result stays with you.
  • Removed the Finalized tag from a finished character. It labelled a state you can see at a glance.
  • Removed the Character heading and the rule beneath it from the first block of the sheet. Naming that block "Character" on a character sheet said nothing. Every other block keeps its heading.
  • The Share Code section is now just Copy Code, Save Local, the character count and the copied confirmation. The code itself is no longer printed on the page — nobody read 13 kB of it, and showing it cost a fold-out panel and two more rules.
  • Copying still works the same way, and now tells you if your browser blocks it instead of looking like it worked.
  • Requires extension 0.9.4. Install link unchanged, so no reinstall — but reload the room once.
ext 0.9.3 Drag the panel to resize it, and three kinds of roll

An extension release. The character creator is unchanged and stays at v.1.23.

  • The roller panel resizes by dragging the strip at its foot. 0.9.2 added − and + buttons in the log header instead, which nobody found and which is not what resizable means. The buttons are still there, next to the grip, for anyone who prefers them. Your chosen height is remembered.
  • Rolls now have three modes instead of a Hidden tick-box. Open is the usual thing — the table sees the roll and the result. Hidden tells the table that you rolled and nothing else: the dice, the target and the outcome stay with you. Secret sends nothing at all, so no one can tell a roll happened.
  • Anyone can roll Hidden. It used to be the GM's alone. Concealing a result is ordinary play, and the table still sees that a roll was made, so nothing goes missing from the record.
  • Secret stays the GM's, on purpose. A player rolling with no trace at all is the one thing a shared log exists to prevent.
  • Concealed rolls are labelled by kind in your own log — amber for Hidden, orange for Secret — so you can always tell what the table was told.
  • Install link unchanged, so no reinstall. Reload the room once.
v.1.23 Sentinels rejoin the party, saved characters attach, and the roller gets rearranged
  • Sentinel characters were invisible to the Party panel. Every other archetype worked. Sentinel's internal code is SNT, and the extension read those first two letters as the start of the character summary it was looking for, then gave up on the whole character as unreadable. Importing the same character into the creator always worked, which is what made it look random.
  • Loading a locally saved character while a sheet was open on a token did not attach it. It rendered fine, had no header bar, and only stuck if you pressed Edit and then Finalize. Loading now attaches exactly like pasting a code does.
  • Players can pay Threat again. Version 1.22 locked all Threat changes to the GM, which was wrong: Nanobarrier, Adrenaline Rush and several items charge Threat, and those are player actions. Adding Threat is open to everyone again; only the GM can spend it back down.
  • The manual Threat + and − buttons on the sheet are now the GM's only, matching the roller. For a player they had stopped working while still announcing themselves in the log.
  • Hidden rolls survive closing the roller. They used to be discarded, so a GM who looked away and came back found them gone, which read as hidden rolls working only sometimes. They are still never sent to the table and never stored in the room.
  • A hidden roll now carries a clear Hidden badge and a tinted row instead of the word "hidden" tacked onto the character's name.
  • The roller is reordered: Table Controls, then Party, then the dice, then the log. A result now sits directly under the button that produced it instead of two panels away.
  • The panel is taller, and there is a height control in the log header. Your choice is remembered. The log and the party list grow with it rather than staying a fixed few lines.
  • The GM can open any character's sheet straight from their row in the Party panel.
  • Scrollbars are themed to match everything else.
  • Requires extension 0.9.2. The install link is unchanged, so no reinstall — but reload the room once.
v.1.22 The GM's controls are the GM's, and no third-party code at the table
  • Rests, scene boundaries and clearing the shared log are now checked against who actually sent them. Before this, the check that you were the GM ran only inside your own window, so anyone at the table with a browser console could call a Bed rest on every character, wipe the Rolls and Actions log, or move Threat. Nothing was wrong with the buttons; there was simply nothing behind them.
  • Nobody needs to have done this for it to have mattered. A forced rest reaches every attached character, including players who are not online, and there is no undo.
  • Momentum is unchanged and stays open to everyone. It is the group's pool.
  • The extension no longer downloads the Owlbear toolkit from a third-party site each time you open a room. It ships with the extension instead, the way the character creator has done since v1.14. Anything that site served would have run with full access to every character at the table.
  • Entries in the shared log are now bounded before they are stored. An oversized one could previously exceed the room's storage limit, which is shared with every other extension you have installed, or freeze everyone's browser as it drew.
  • A character code that has been tampered with can no longer alter how the sheet handles the character object it builds.
  • Requires extension 0.9.1. The install link is unchanged, so no reinstall is needed — but everyone should reload the room once.
v.1.21 Characters stay on their tokens, and a sheet in play can be edited
  • Building a brand-new character straight onto a token now sticks. Pressing Finalize saves it to the token the way pasting a code always has. Before this it looked finished, played correctly, and was gone the moment you closed the sheet, because it had never actually been written to the token.
  • This is also why the GM's Party panel could read empty and why rests and scene boundaries never arrived: both go looking for characters on tokens, and a character that was never saved is not there to find.
  • A finished character can now be edited from inside Owlbear. The step bar — Origin, Archetype, Growth and the rest — appears when you press Edit Character, so you can go straight to the step you want.
  • Added a Spend Growth button to the Growth section of the sheet. When the GM grants Growth mid-session, that is one click to the right screen instead of hunting for it.
  • While editing a finished character, every step offers "Done — back to play", so you are never walked through the whole wizard to get back to the sheet. Returning to play changes nothing you have spent or taken.
  • A rest the GM called while you were part-way through editing now lands as soon as you press Done, rather than waiting for the next thing to happen in the room.
  • Extension 0.9.0 is unchanged and still required. No reinstall.
v.1.20 Injuries in the log, a party panel for the GM, and a sheet in five parts
  • Taking, healing, reopening, changing or removing an Injury now posts to the Rolls and Actions log, so the table sees who got hurt and who got patched up. Damage was the last consequential thing at the table that happened silently.
  • Injuries still have no mechanical effect of their own. Nothing here changes a number: an Injury is a Truth representing harm, and the Spirit you spend to avoid one was already logged.
  • Renaming an Injury — which is how the rules treat one, turning Bleeding into Bandaged Wound — posts once when you finish typing, not once per letter.
  • Heal and Treat are the same control. Pressing Heal moves the Injury into Healed Injuries and says so in the log.
  • Added a Party panel to the roller, visible only to the GM. It lists every character on a token in the scene with their Spirit and whether they have caught up with the rests and scene boundaries you have called.
  • A character reads as Caught up, Behind, or Not synced. Behind names what it is waiting on, so "Waiting on Bed" tells you who to chase. Not synced means the character has never met this room and has nothing to catch up on — a new character is not behind.
  • Opening the roller with a token already selected now reads that token straight away. It used to show nothing until you clicked something else.
  • The play sheet is now five clearly separated sections: Character, Actions Talents & Abilities, Items and Equipment, Growth, and Share Code. Nothing moved relative to anything else — the same sections in the same order, grouped so the page reads as five things instead of one long column.
  • Character Share Code is its own titled section at the foot of the sheet.
  • Starting Equipment and Item Catalogue now start collapsed. Open one and it stays open while you work.
  • Removed the Shared Roll Room. It needed a server that was never set up, and its Connect button could only ever fail. Rolls have reached the table through Owlbear since v1.12, which needs no server at all.
  • Requires extension 0.9.0. The install link is unchanged, so no reinstall is needed.
v.1.19B Nanobarrier fix and catch-up improvements
  • Fixed Nanobarrier doing nothing. Sentinels are granted it automatically, and the sheet was not counting an automatically granted talent as one you have, so every press was silently ignored and the cost never moved off Free.
  • Sheets now catch up the moment they are opened, instead of waiting until you rolled or pressed something.
  • When your sheet catches up, it posts to the log so the table can see who has received a rest and who has not.
  • The catch-up entry carries the detail — Spirit regained, items recharged — rather than only naming what the GM called.
  • A rest that restores no Spirit no longer says "regained 0 Spirit".
  • GM table control buttons now ask for confirmation. The first press arms the button, the second sends it, and it disarms itself after a few seconds.
  • Adjusting Momentum or Threat with the roller's own buttons now posts to the log, attributed to whoever pressed them.
  • Requires extension 0.8.1.
v.1.19 GM table controls
  • Added a Table Controls panel to the roller, visible only to the GM, with buttons for Breather, Break and Bed rests and for End Scene, New Session and New Adventure.
  • Pressing one applies it to every attached character, not just the sheets that happen to be open.
  • A character whose sheet was closed catches up the next time it is opened, and is told what was called.
  • Catching up after several missed presses applies the strongest one once. Three missed Bed rests is one Bed rest.
  • A new character attached to a token does not retroactively take rests it was never present for.
  • The GM calling a rest appears once in the log, attributed to the GM, rather than once per character.
  • Requires extension 0.8.0. The install link is unchanged, so no reinstall is needed.
v.1.18 Nanobarrier, damage buttons, and item charge logging
  • Added Nanobarrier as a tracked ability with all three options — Barrier, Shock and Display — each showing what the next activation will cost.
  • Nanobarrier's first use in a scene is free, the second costs 1 Threat, and each use after that costs one more. All three options draw on the same count.
  • The cost resets at End Scene, and therefore also at New Session and New Adventure.
  • Replaced the single extra-damage button with six, one per Momentum, labelled so both the cost and the damage are visible before you commit.
  • Damage buttons show doubled damage for characters with Precision Firepower, with a note naming the talent responsible.
  • Discharging or recharging a Powered item now posts to the Rolls and Actions log.
  • A rest posts a single summary line rather than one entry per item recharged.
v.1.17 Rolls and Actions log, item buttons, and Start Over
  • The shared roll log became the Rolls and Actions log: it now records ability uses, item uses, and every change to the Momentum and Threat pools alongside rolls.
  • Log entries name who acted and what they used, so "Kell — Second Wind — spent 3 Momentum, regained 2 Spirit" reads as one line instead of a number moving on its own.
  • Added buttons for the five items whose rules carry a cost: Communicator, Tactical Lens, Combat Automed and Emergency Trauma Kit add Threat; Combat Medkit spends Momentum. These were previously notes asking you to move the counter yourself.
  • An item button you cannot currently afford is shown greyed out rather than hidden, so you can still see the item has the ability.
  • Manual adjustments to the Threat counter now post to the log.
  • Added a Start Over button beside Save Local on a finished sheet. It warns first if the character has not been saved locally.
  • Moved Starting Equipment above the Item Catalogue in Inventory & Equipment.
  • Removed the Shared Roll Room panel, which relied on a server that is not going to be available.
  • Confirmed that New Adventure refreshes I Know a Guy, which it already did.

Owlbear Rodeo

v.1.16 One sheet, and no more loading from elsewhere
  • The Owlbear character sheet and the character creator became a single page, so the sheet you play on and the sheet you build on can no longer drift apart.
  • Fixed: opening a character from a token could quietly fall back to the ordinary creator, showing the import screen instead of your character. Anything you changed was saved to your browser rather than to the token, and your rolls and Momentum never reached the table.
  • Fixed: rolls made from the sheet were reaching the shared log without their Difficulty, so they always read as if you had rolled against Difficulty 0.
  • The creator is now completely self-contained. It loads nothing from anywhere, which means it also works offline and from a saved copy on your own machine.
v.1.15 Threat goes where it is supposed to go
  • Adrenaline Rush and I Know a Guy no longer just tell you to add Threat. Played inside Owlbear Rodeo, they add it to the table's Threat pool for you.
  • There is a Threat counter beside your other resources when you are playing on a token, showing the table's current Threat. Use the plus button for anything else that tells you to add Threat, such as a Communicator message or a Tactical Lens signal.
  • Momentum on an Owlbear sheet is the group's pool, not a private counter. Spending it in any way — the buttons, the number field, or an ability — moves the shared total.

In a normal browser tab nothing changes: the sheet still tells you the number and leaves the pool to your Game Master, because there is no shared table to write to.

v.1.14 Quicker rolling, and dice that tell you the result
  • Click any Attribute or Skill to select it for a test. The one you picked is highlighted, and an Attribute shut down by Exhaustion cannot be picked.
  • The Dice Roller moved up to sit directly beneath your Attributes, Skills and Resources, instead of near the bottom of the sheet.
  • There is a Difficulty setting next to the Roll button. Your result now says whether the test passed and how much Momentum you gained, rather than leaving you to work it out.
  • The share code block leads with Copy Code and Save Local, and the code itself sits in a small scrolling box below rather than filling the page.
  • Momentum earned on a roll is reported, not spent for you. You can use it straight away or save it to the group pool, up to the usual maximum of 6.
v.1.13 The last of the sheet travels with your code
  • Your temperament's Drive and Attitude now travel with your code, alongside the exhaustion trigger that was already there.
  • Your archetype goal, your starting equipment list and your origin's special note come across too.
  • Coin, Growth and Momentum maxima are included, along with the "equipped +N" footnote that explains where a raised maximum came from.
  • Powered items carry their recharge timing, so a tabletop can tell a Breather item from a Bed one.
  • Codes stopped growing with your inventory. They sit at roughly 10 kB no matter how much gear you carry.
v.1.12 Abilities, descriptions and exhaustion in your code
  • Exhaustion states travel with your code. A tabletop that reads it can show all four, let you toggle them, and grey out the attribute each one shuts down.
  • Your origin abilities now come across with their full rules text, so you can read what they do without opening the creator.
  • Origin, archetype and temperament descriptions are included, along with your temperament's exhaustion clause.
  • Bonds now carry the bond type's name and description rather than just a label.
  • Codes grew to around 9 kB. Older codes still work; you just will not get exhaustion tracking until you export a fresh one.
v.1.11 Character codes a virtual tabletop can read
  • Your code now includes your finished numbers: attributes, skills, Spirit and Supply maxima, tech level, your talents and your equipment with damage, injury, protection and qualities.
  • This means another tool can display your character without needing a copy of the rules. Paste your code in once and it knows who you are.
  • Everything you change during play — Spirit spent, injuries taken, truths written, gear discharged — can be carried back here by pasting the code in again. Nothing is lost in the trip.
  • Codes roughly doubled in length. Older codes still load normally, and codes made from this version onward still work in older copies of the creator.
